20.08.2026

A delicious start to the school year!

From September 1, every I'm restaurant in Kazakhstan offers a special school combo: a Chicken Burger, small fries, and a soft drink for only 1150 ₸. The promotion runs until September 30 and is available in all cities where I'm operates.

Come with your friends, collect bonus points on every order, and start the school year with something tasty. The combo is available for dine-in, takeaway, and delivery.

TASTE OR CLICK?

Ordering through the I'm mobile app just got more rewarding. Every purchase earns you bonus points — 1 point for every 100 ₸ spent. Points accumulate automatically and can be redeemed for free dishes and full combos.

Download the app, register with your phone number, and get 100 welcome points on your first order. The app is available for iOS and Android, and works in every I'm restaurant across Kazakhstan.

15.08.2026

Buy fries — help children!

Throughout September, part of the proceeds from every large portion of French Fries goes to a children's charity fund in Astana. The campaign is called "September 22" and aims to support kids from low-income families with school supplies.

By ordering fries, you're not just getting a tasty side — you're helping a child start the school year with everything they need. The campaign runs in all I'm restaurants until September 30.

12.08.2026

Meet the new I'm in Almaty!

A new I'm restaurant has opened at Alma-Ata Plaza in Almaty. The first 100 guests received exclusive gifts and a 20% discount on their first order. The restaurant is open every day from 10:00 to 23:00.

The new location features the full I'm menu — burgers, combos, sides, desserts, and drinks. It also has a spacious seating area for families and a pickup counter for app orders.

10.08.2026

Happy Birthday, Almaty!

From September 18 to 24, all residents and guests of Almaty get a 10% discount on any order at every I'm restaurant in the city. The promotion celebrates Almaty's birthday and is our way of saying thank you to the city that welcomed us.

The discount applies to dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders. No promo code needed — just mention the promotion when ordering or select it in the app.

05.08.2026

New bonus program is live!

Our brand-new bonus program has launched across all I'm restaurants. Every order earns you points, and every 100 points can be redeemed for free dishes or a full combo. Points are saved to your account automatically — no card needed.

To start collecting, simply register on the site or in the app and place your first order. You'll see your current balance in the "My Account" section, along with your order history and progress toward your next free meal.
