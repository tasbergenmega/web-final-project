About I'm

A Kazakh fast-food brand born from a big story — juicy burgers, crispy fries, and honest service.

Our Story

The I'M Restaurants brand in Kazakhstan began after the departure of the international McDonald's chain — and the renaming of the company's former restaurants.

On November 23, 2023, entrepreneur Kairat Boranbayev unveiled the new Kazakhstani brand I'M, bringing together the team's many years of experience in the fast-food industry.

Today I'm is a fast-food restaurant chain offering quality dishes made from fresh ingredients. Our goal is fast service, great taste, and a comfortable atmosphere for every guest.
